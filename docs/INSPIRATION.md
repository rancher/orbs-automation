# Project Gero (`gero`)

> Automating workflows, orchestrating tasks, and powering team ORBS.

---

## The Inspiration

Our team name—**ORBS**—was inspired by the legendary **Dragon Balls** from *Dragon Ball Z*. In that same spirit, our CLI automation tool is named **`gero`**, after **Dr. Gero**, the genius scientist behind the Red Ribbon Army's autonomous research division.

### Why Dr. Gero?

* **Pioneer of Synthetic Automation:** Dr. Gero’s life's work was designing autonomous, self-executing systems (Androids) engineered to carry out complex tasks without manual intervention.
* **Continuous Integration & Evolution:** Gero built autonomous spy drones to continuously monitor activity, gather operational data, and refine his creations—the exact goal of modern CI/CD, telemetry, and background automation.
* **Reliability & Power:** Like Gero's infinite energy models, a good engineering CLI acts as an unyielding engine that takes care of heavy operational lifting, boilerplate generation, and pipeline orchestration.

---

## Command Line Experience

While the tool's name pays homage to DBZ lore, the CLI interface itself remains pragmatic, clean, and intuitive for daily developer workflows using standard `gero <category> <action>` syntax.

### Commands

The commands are noun and verb, as in `gh`: `gero repo status`, `gero ship`, `gero lines resolve`, `gero config validate`, `gero doctor`. `gero --help` is the current tree, and [CLI_FRAMEWORK](specs/CLI_FRAMEWORK.md) is how it is organized.

---

## Summary

**`gero`** brings autonomous engineering directly to team ORBS's terminal—eliminating manual toil, speeding up developer velocity, and unifying our workflows under a single, 4-character binary.
